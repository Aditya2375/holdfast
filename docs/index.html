04 Know the edges

            A CONTAINER IS NOT A VM

              Trusted root, shared kernel. Supply your own quiescent
              local tree, including its ancestors. Read-only mounts do not stop
              host-side changes. No claim of safety against hostile workloads or
              kernel bugs.
            

            

              One process, intentionally. No forks, threads, sockets,
              image pulling, OCI support, cgroups, devices or interactive
              terminal. Global kernel metrics may remain visible. stdout/stderr
              are deliberate writable host endpoints.
            

            

              Bounds, not a VM budget. rlimits are per process, not
              cgroup accounting. No output-byte cap. Inherited supplementary
              host groups are not cleared. A stalled kernel can delay
              termination. Use a VM for untrusted code.
